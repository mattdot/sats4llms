# Structured Analytic Techniques for Intelligence Analysis (Book)

[entity_type::book]

**Authors:** [[entities/richards-j-heuer-jr|Richards j. heuer jr.]] & [[entities/randolph-h-pherson|Randolph H. Pherson]]  
**Publisher:** SAGE/College Publishing  
**Edition cited:** 3rd edition

The primary book-length reference for the full SAT toolkit. Broader in scope than the [[sources/tradecraft-primer-2009|CIA Tradecraft Primer (2009)]], which covers only 12 techniques. Includes [[concepts/starbursting|Starbursting]] and other techniques not in the Primer.

This is the methodology reference used by [[entities/scott-roberts|Scott Roberts]] in his LLM-SAT implementation work.

---

## Relationship to the CIA Tradecraft Primer

The [[sources/tradecraft-primer-2009|CIA Tradecraft Primer (2009)]] is a condensed practitioner guide. The Heuer/Pherson book is the full academic/practitioner reference. As of this wiki's current sources, the book has not been directly ingested — it is known only through secondary reference in [[sources/roberts-llm-sats-ftw-2025|Roberts: LLM SATs FTW (2025)]].
