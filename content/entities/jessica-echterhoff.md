# Jessica Maria Echterhoff

Researcher at UC San Diego. Lead author of the 2024 BiasBuster study — the most comprehensive single empirical investigation of multiple cognitive biases in LLM decision-making, and the closest existing analog to the empirical program proposed on this wiki's [[synthesis/sat-llm-hypotheses|hypotheses page]].
