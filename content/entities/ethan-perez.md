# Ethan Perez

Alignment researcher at [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]. Author or co-author of multiple foundational papers on LLM behavioral measurement, including the first at-scale measurement of [[concepts/sycophancy|sycophancy]] and inverse-scaling behaviors, and contributor to the [[sources/kadavath-know-2022|Kadavath self-knowledge study]] and the [[sources/sharma-sycophancy-2023|Sharma sycophancy paper]].
