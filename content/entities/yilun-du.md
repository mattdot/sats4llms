# Yilun Du

Researcher at MIT CSAIL. Lead author of the foundational 2023 multi-agent debate paper, the primary empirical evidence that multi-instance critique pipelines improve LLM factuality and reasoning — directly relevant to [[synthesis/sat-llm-hypotheses|H5]] on this wiki.
